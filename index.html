About Us

A studio built on craft, restraint and results.

We are NOVARA.

Founded in 2021, NOVARA is a focused team of designers, engineers and strategists obsessed with the craft of the modern web. We build for founders, agencies and enterprises who treat their website as their most important salesperson.

We do fewer projects, deeply. Every engagement is led by a senior partner. Every interface is hand-built. Every line of code is performance-budgeted.

The result: websites that load instantly, convert relentlessly, and look like nothing else in your category.
